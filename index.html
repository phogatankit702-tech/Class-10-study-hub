Practice MCQ:
1. $Fe_2O_3 + 2Al \rightarrow Al_2O_3 + 2Fe$ is an example of a:
(a) Combination reaction
(b) Double displacement reaction
(c) Decomposition reaction
(d) Displacement reaction
View Correct Answer

Answer: (d) Displacement reaction. Aluminum displaces iron from iron oxide because it is more reactive.

10. Myopia (near-sightedness) can be corrected by using a:
(a) Convex lens
(b) Concave lens
(c) Bifocal lens
(d) Cylindrical lens
View Correct Answer

Answer: (b) Concave lens. A diverging concave lens corrects myopic focus back onto the retina.

  • Factors affecting resistance & resistivity ($\rho$).

Practice MCQ:
11. If two resistors of $6\,\Omega$ each are connected in parallel, the equivalent resistance is:
(a) $12\,\Omega$
(b) $6\,\Omega$
(c) $3\,\Omega$
(d) $1.5\,\Omega$
View Correct Answer

Answer: (c) $3\,\Omega$. $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3} \implies R_p = 3\,\Omega$.

13. In a food chain, what percentage of solar energy trapped by green plants is converted into food energy?
