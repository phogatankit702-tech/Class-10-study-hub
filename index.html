📄 PAGE 1: Science - Chemistry & Biology Core Formulas

Chemical Reactions: Conservation of mass mandates balanced equations. Oxidation = Gain of $O_2$ / Loss of $H_2$. Reduction = Gain of $H_2$ / Loss of $O_2$.
Acids, Bases & Salts: $\text{pH} < 7$ (Acidic), $\text{pH} = 7$ (Neutral), $\text{pH} > 7$ (Basic). Bleaching Powder ($\text{CaOCl}_2$), Baking Soda ($\text{NaHCO}_3$), Washing Soda ($\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3 \cdot 10\text{H}_2\text{O}$), Plaster of Paris ($\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$).
Life Processes: Aerobic respiration produces 38 ATP. Double circulation in human heart prevents mixing of oxygenated & deoxygenated blood. Nephrons act as functional filtration units in kidneys.

📄 PAGE 2: Science - Physics Laws & Formula Cheat Sheet

Light Reflection & Refraction: Mirror formula $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$. Lens formula $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$. Lens Power $P = \frac{1}{f\text{ (m)}}$ (Dioptre, D).
Electricity & Magnetism: Ohm's Law $V = IR$. Series $R_s = R_1 + R_2 + R_3$. Parallel $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3}$. Heating effect $H = I^2Rt$. Fleming's Left-Hand Rule predicts magnetic force direction.

📄 PAGE 3: Mathematics - Complete Formula Booklet

Real Numbers & Polynomials: $\text{HCF} \times \text{LCM} = a \times b$. Sum of zeros $\alpha+\beta = -\frac{b}{a}$, Product $\alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}$.
Quadratic & AP: Quadratic formula $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$. AP $n^{\text{th}}$ term $a_n = a + (n-1)d$; Sum $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$.
Trigonometry & Mensuration: $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$. Volume of sphere $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, Cone $\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$, Cylinder $\pi r^2 h$.
