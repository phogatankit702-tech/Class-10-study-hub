SCIENCE - CHAPTER 1

Chemical Reactions and Equations

Chapter 1 PDF Notes (3 Pages)
⚡ Important Chemical Equations & Formulas
  • Combination: $2\text{Mg} + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{MgO}$
  • Decomposition: $2\text{FeSO}_4 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + \text{SO}_2 + \text{SO}_3$
  • Displacement: $\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4 \rightarrow \text{FeSO}_4 + \text{Cu}$

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. Chemical Change: A process where reactants transform into new substances with different properties. Indicators include evolution of gas, change in color, change in temperature, or formation of a precipitate.

2. Law of Conservation of Mass: Matter can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. The total mass of elements on the reactant side must equal the product side.

3. Reaction Types: Combination (two combine into one), Decomposition (one breaks down into multiple), Single Displacement (reactive element displaces weaker one), Double Displacement (ion exchange), and Redox (simultaneous oxidation and reduction).

MATHEMATICS - CHAPTER 4

Quadratic Equations

Chapter 4 PDF Notes (3 Pages)
⚡ Essential Formulas
  • Standard Form: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0 \quad (a \neq 0)$
  • Quadratic Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
  • Discriminant: $D = b^2 - 4ac$

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. Standard Definition: A second-degree polynomial equation of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$. The roots of the equation are the values of $x$ that satisfy it.

2. Methods of Solution: Factorization (splitting the middle term) and the Quadratic Formula.

3. Nature of Roots (Discriminant D):

  • If $D > 0$: Two distinct real roots.
  • If $D = 0$: Two equal real roots ($x = -b/2a$).
  • If $D < 0$: No real roots (imaginary roots).
SOCIAL SCIENCE - HISTORY CH 2

Nationalism in India

History Ch 2 PDF Notes (3 Pages)

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. The First World War & Satyagraha: World War I led to economic hardship in India. Mahatma Gandhi returned to India in 1915 and introduced Satyagraha—a method based on truth and non-violence (Champaran, Kheda, Ahmedabad).

2. Rowlatt Act & Jallianwala Bagh (1919): The Rowlatt Act permitted detention without trial. On April 13, 1919, General Dyer opened fire on peaceful demonstrators at Jallianwala Bagh, Amritsar.

3. Non-Cooperation Movement (1921-1922): Launched jointly with the Khilafat Movement. Boycotts of foreign cloth, schools, and courts took place. It was called off after the Chauri Chaura violence in 1922.

4. Civil Disobedience Movement (1930): Started with the famous Dandi Salt March (380 km from Sabarmati to Dandi). Ended with the Gandhi-Irwin Pact in 1931.

ENGLISH - FIRST FLIGHT CH 1

A Letter to God

English Ch 1 PDF Notes (3 Pages)

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. Plot Overview: Lencho, a hardworking farmer, expects a good harvest from his cornfield. However, a severe hailstorm completely destroys his crop, leaving his family on the verge of starvation.

2. Unshakable Faith: Possessing firm belief in God, Lencho writes a letter to God requesting 100 pesos to re-sow his field and sustain his family.

3. Postmaster's Gesture: The postmaster reads the letter and decides to help to keep Lencho's faith alive. He collects 70 pesos from post office employees and sends it back in an envelope signed 'God'.

4. Irony of the Ending: Upon receiving 70 pesos instead of 100, Lencho suspects the post office staff of stealing the remaining 30 pesos and calls them a "bunch of crooks."

Hindi Ch 1 PDF Notes (3 Pages)

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. मुख्य कहानी: हालदार साहब हर पंद्रहवें दिन कस्बे से गुजरते थे, जहाँ चौराहे पर नेताजी सुभाषचंद्र बोस की संगमरमर की मूर्ति लगी थी। मूर्तिकार मूर्ति का चश्मा बनाना भूल गया था।

2. कैप्टन चश्मेवाला: कस्बे का एक देशभक्त, जिसे लोग 'कैप्टन' कहते थे, रोज मूर्ति पर नया असली चश्मा लगा देता था। हालदार साहब उसकी देशभक्ति से बहुत प्रभावित हुए।

3. कैप्टन की मृत्यु और अंत: कैप्टन की मृत्यु के बाद मूर्ति बिना चश्मे के रह गई। लेकिन कुछ दिनों बाद हालदार साहब ने देखा कि मूर्ति पर सरकंडे का छोटा चश्मा लगा था, जिसे बच्चों ने बनाया था। यह देखकर हालदार साहब भावुक हो गए कि नई पीढ़ी में भी देशभक्ति जीवित है।
