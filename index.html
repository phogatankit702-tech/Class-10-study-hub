SCIENCE - CHAPTER 1

Chemical Reactions and Equations

Chapter 1 PDF Notes (3 Pages)
⚡ Important Chemical Equations & Formulas
  • Combination: $2\text{Mg} + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{MgO}$
  • Decomposition: $2\text{FeSO}_4 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + \text{SO}_2 + \text{SO}_3$
  • Displacement: $\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4 \rightarrow \text{FeSO}_4 + \text{Cu}$

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. Chemical Changes: Involves formation of new chemical substances. Accompanied by temperature shifts, gas evolution, color changes, or precipitate formation.

2. Law of Conservation of Mass: Total mass of reactants must equal total mass of products. Equation balancing is mandatory on both sides.

3. Reaction Classification: Combination (2 -> 1), Decomposition (1 -> 2+), Displacement (More active element displaces weaker one), Double Displacement (Ion exchange), and Redox (Simultaneous oxidation and reduction).

SCIENCE - CHAPTER 2

Acids, Bases and Salts

Chapter 2 PDF Notes (3 Pages)
⚡ Chemical Formulas of Salts
  • Bleaching Powder: $\text{CaOCl}_2$
  • Baking Soda: $\text{NaHCO}_3$
  • Washing Soda: $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3 \cdot 10\text{H}_2\text{O}$
  • Plaster of Paris: $\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. Indicators & pH Scale: Acids release $H^+$ ions ($\text{pH} < 7$), turning blue litmus red. Bases release $OH^-$ ions ($\text{pH} > 7$), turning red litmus blue. Universal indicators measure precise hydrogen ion concentrations.

2. Neutralization Reaction: $\text{Acid} + \text{Base} \rightarrow \text{Salt} + \text{Water}$. Acids reacting with metals produce hydrogen gas ($\text{H}_2$).

3. Salts in Daily Life: Sodium hydroxide ($\text{NaOH}$) via Chlor-alkali process, Plaster of Paris forming Gypsum upon adding water.

MATHEMATICS - CHAPTER 1

Real Numbers

Chapter 1 PDF Notes (3 Pages)
⚡ Fundamental Formulas
  • HCF $\times$ LCM = $a \times b$
  • Irrationality Proof: $\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5}$

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: Every composite number can be uniquely expressed as a product of prime numbers, apart from the order of prime factors.

2. HCF & LCM Relation: For any two positive integers $a$ and $b$, $\text{HCF}(a,b) \times \text{LCM}(a,b) = a \times b$.

3. Proof of Irrationality: Uses proof by contradiction to establish that numbers like $\sqrt{p}$ (where $p$ is prime) cannot be written as $p/q$.

MATHEMATICS - CHAPTER 4

Quadratic Equations

Chapter 4 PDF Notes (3 Pages)
⚡ Essential Formulas
  • Standard Form: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$
  • Quadratic Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
  • Discriminant: $D = b^2 - 4ac$

1-Page Chapter Summary

1. Standard Form: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a \neq 0$.

2. Methods of Solving: Factorization (splitting middle term) and Quadratic Formula.

3. Discriminant ($D = b^2 - 4ac$): If $D > 0$, 2 distinct real roots. If $D = 0$, 2 equal real roots. If $D < 0$, no real roots.
