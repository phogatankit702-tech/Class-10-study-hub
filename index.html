🧪 Science (Key Concepts)

  1. Law of Conservation of Mass: Total mass of reactants equals total mass of products in a chemical reaction.
  2. Exothermic vs Endothermic: Exothermic releases heat ($\text{C} + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO}_2 + \text{heat}$); Endothermic absorbs heat.
  3. Redox Reaction: Simultaneous oxidation (gain of $\text{O}_2$/loss of $\text{H}_2$) and reduction (loss of $\text{O}_2$/gain of $\text{H}_2$).
  4. pH Scale: $< 7$ is acidic, $= 7$ is neutral, $> 7$ is basic. Human blood pH range is $7.0 - 7.8$.
  5. Plaster of Paris: $\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$ hardens into Gypsum ($\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$) when mixed with water.
  6. Reactivity Series: Potassium ($\text{K}$) is most reactive, while Gold ($\text{Au}$) is least reactive.
  7. Ionic Compounds: High melting points due to strong electrostatic forces between oppositely charged ions.
  8. Catenation: Unique property of carbon to form long chains with other carbon atoms.
  9. Isomers: Compounds with same molecular formula but different structural arrangements.
  10. Photosynthesis Equation: $6\text{CO}_2 + 12\text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{\text{Sunlight/Chlorophyll}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\text{O}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$.
  11. Aerobic vs Anaerobic: Aerobic occurs in mitochondria (38 ATP); Anaerobic occurs in cytoplasm (2 ATP).
  12. Double Circulation: Blood travels through the human heart twice during one complete cycle.
  13. Synapse: Microscopic gap between two adjacent neurons where signals cross via chemicals.
  14. Plant Hormones: Auxin (cell elongation), Cytokinin (cell division), Abscisic acid (growth inhibitor).
  15. Reflex Arc: Pathway taken by nerve impulses in a reflex action (Receptor $\rightarrow$ Sensory Neuron $\rightarrow$ Spinal Cord $\rightarrow$ Motor Neuron $\rightarrow$ Effector).

📐 Mathematics (Important Formulas & Theorems)

  1. Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic: Every composite number can be uniquely factorized into prime factors.
  2. HCF and LCM Relation: $\text{HCF}(a, b) \times \text{LCM}(a, b) = a \times b$.
  3. Quadratic Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$. Discriminant $D = b^2 - 4ac$.
  4. Nature of Roots: If $D > 0$ (two real distinct roots), $D = 0$ (two equal real roots), $D < 0$ (no real roots).
  5. Arithmetic Progression (AP): $n^{\text{th}}$ term $a_n = a + (n-1)d$; Sum $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$.
  6. Distance Formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$.
  7. Section Formula: $P(x,y) = \left(\frac{m_1x_2 + m_2x_1}{m_1 + m_2}, \frac{m_1y_2 + m_2y_1}{m_1 + m_2}\right)$.
  8. Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT): A line drawn parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio.
  9. Trigonometric Identity 1: $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$.
  10. Trigonometric Identity 2: $1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta$.
  11. Trigonometric Identity 3: $1 + \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta$.
  12. Tangent to a Circle: Perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.
  13. Length of Tangents: Tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal in length.
  14. Area of Sector: $\text{Area} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2$.
  15. Empirical Formula (Statistics): $\text{Mode} = 3(\text{Median}) - 2(\text{Mean})$.

🌍 Social Science (History, Geography, Civics & Economics)

  1. Nationalism in Europe: Giuseppe Mazzini founded 'Young Italy' in 1831; Otto von Bismarck unified Germany.
  2. Satyagraha Movements: Champaran (1917 - Indigo farmers), Kheda (1917 - Peasant crop failure), Ahmedabad (1918 - Cotton mill workers).
  3. Jallianwala Bagh Massacre: Happened on 13th April 1919 in Amritsar under General Dyer's command.
  4. Non-Cooperation Movement: Launched in 1920, called off in February 1922 due to the Chauri Chaura violence.
  5. Civil Disobedience Movement: Started with Dandi Salt March (12 March – 6 April 1930) covering 240 miles.
  6. Resource Classification: Biotic/Abiotic, Renewable/Non-renewable, National/International.
  7. Soil Types: Alluvial (most fertile/Northern plains), Black soil (Cotton cultivation/Regur soil), Red & Yellow, Laterite.
  8. Major Crops: Rice (Kharif, staple crop), Wheat (Rabi, cool growing season), Sugarcane (Tropical/Subtropical).
  9. Power Sharing: Essential to prevent conflict; Majoritarianism in Sri Lanka led to civil war, whereas Belgium's power-sharing brought stability.
  10. Federalism: Division of power between Union, State, and Local governments (3-tier system in India since 1992).
  11. Development Index: Per Capita Income = $\frac{\text{Total Income of Country}}{\text{Total Population}}$. Published in UNDP's Human Development Report.
  12. Sectors of Economy: Primary (Agriculture/Mining), Secondary (Manufacturing/Industry), Tertiary (Services/Transportation).
  13. Credit Sources: Formal (Banks/SHGs - low interest) vs Informal (Moneylenders/Landlords - high interest).
  14. Globalization: Integration of national markets enabled by MNCs, technology, and trade liberalization.
  15. Self-Help Groups (SHGs): Typically consist of 15–20 members pooling savings to offer low-interest microloans.

📖 English (Grammar Rules & Summaries)

  1. A Letter to God: Lencho, a faithful farmer, writes to God for 100 pesos after hailstorms destroy his crops; receives 70 pesos collected by post office staff.
  2. Nelson Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom: Highlights the struggle against Apartheid and inauguration of South Africa's first democratic non-racial government in 1994.
  3. Two Stories About Flying: Part I (Young seagull overcomes fear of first flight); Part II (Black Aeroplane guides pilot safely through storm).
  4. From the Diary of Anne Frank: Anne names her diary 'Kitty' to share thoughts while hiding in Amsterdam during WWII.
  5. Dust of Snow (Poem): A crow shaking hemlock tree snow onto Frost transforms his gloomy mood into a positive state.
  6. Fire and Ice (Poem): Fire symbolizes desire/lust; Ice symbolizes hatred/indifference; both can destroy the world.
  7. A Triumph of Surgery: Dr. Herriot cures Tricki (overfed dog) purely through strict diet and physical exercise without medical surgery.
  8. The Thief's Story: Hari Singh (15-year-old thief) decides not to rob Anil because of Anil's trust, honesty, and effort to teach him to read.
  9. Subject-Verb Agreement: Singular subjects require singular verbs; plural subjects require plural verbs.
  10. Direct to Indirect Speech: Present tense changes to corresponding past tense if the reporting verb is in the past tense.
  11. Modals Usage: 'Must' indicates compulsion; 'Should' indicates advice; 'May' indicates formal permission or possibility.
