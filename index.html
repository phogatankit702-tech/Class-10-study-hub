⚡ Essential Chemical Equations & Formulas

  • Decomposition: $2\text{FeSO}_4 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + \text{SO}_2 + \text{SO}_3$
  • Displacement: $\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4 \rightarrow \text{FeSO}_4 + \text{Cu}$

Chapter Summary & Key Concepts

A chemical reaction occurs when one or more substances transform into new substances with entirely different physical and chemical properties. Key signs of a chemical reaction include gas evolution, color changes, temperature variation, or precipitate formation.

Balancing Chemical Equations: According to the Law of Conservation of Mass, matter cannot be created or destroyed in a chemical reaction. Therefore, the total number of atoms of each element must remain equal on both reactant and product sides.

🎯 Competency-Based Practice Questions
Q1. A student adds iron filings to a copper sulfate solution. What color change occurs and why?
View Answer

The blue solution turns light green due to the formation of ferrous sulfate ($\text{FeSO}_4$). Iron displaces copper as it is more reactive.

Q2. Why is respiration considered an exothermic reaction?
View Answer

Glucose reacts with oxygen in body cells to produce carbon dioxide, water, and release energy in the form of ATP.

Q3. What happens chemically when quicklime ($\text{CaO}$) reacts vigorously with water?
View Answer

Slaked lime ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2$) is produced, releasing a significant amount of heat energy.

Q4. Identify the oxidizing agent: $\text{CuO} + \text{H}_2 \rightarrow \text{Cu} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$.
View Answer

$\text{CuO}$ is the oxidizing agent because it supplies oxygen to hydrogen.

Q5. Why are oil and fat-containing food items flushed with nitrogen gas?
View Answer

Nitrogen acts as an inert atmosphere to prevent oxidation and rancidity of oils and fats.

🎯 Competency-Based Practice Questions
Q1. Find the nature of roots for $2x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0$.
View Answer

$D = (-4)^2 - 4(2)(3) = 16 - 24 = -8$. Since $D < 0$, there are no real roots.
