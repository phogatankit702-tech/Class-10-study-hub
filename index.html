Chapter: Motion

Key concepts, formula cheat sheet, and practice quiz.

⚡ Quick Formula Cheat Sheet

  • Speed: $s = \frac{d}{t}$
  • Velocity: $v = \frac{\Delta x}{t}$
  • Acceleration: $a = \frac{v - u}{t}$
  • First Equation of Motion: $v = u + at$

📖 Chapter Notes

1. Distance vs. Displacement

Distance: The total path length covered by an object during motion. It is a scalar quantity (has magnitude only).

Displacement: The shortest straight-line distance from the initial position to the final position. It is a vector quantity (has magnitude and direction).

2. Uniform and Non-Uniform Motion

Uniform Motion: When an object covers equal distances in equal intervals of time.

Non-Uniform Motion: When an object covers unequal distances in equal intervals of time.
