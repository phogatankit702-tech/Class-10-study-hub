Chemical Reactions and Equations

Chapter 1 Text Notes
⚡ Essential Chemical Equations & Formulas
  • Combination: $2\text{Mg} + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{MgO}$
  • Decomposition: $2\text{FeSO}_4 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + \text{SO}_2 + \text{SO}_3$
  • Displacement: $\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4 \rightarrow \text{FeSO}_4 + \text{Cu}$

1-Page Complete Chapter Summary

A chemical reaction represents the transformation of chemical substances into new items possessing distinct chemical properties. Key signs of chemical activity include changes in temperature, color evolution, gas release, or precipitate formation.

Law of Conservation of Mass: Total mass of reactants must equal total mass of products. Therefore, all chemical equations must be strictly balanced on both sides.

Quadratic Equations

Chapter 4 Text Notes
⚡ Important Formulas
  • Standard Form: $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$
  • Quadratic Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
  • Discriminant: $D = b^2 - 4ac$

1-Page Complete Chapter Summary

A quadratic equation in the variable $x$ is an equation of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a, b, c$ are real numbers and $a \neq 0$. The roots of the quadratic equation represent the values of $x$ that satisfy the relation.

Page 2: Life Processes & Control System

Page 3: Physics - Light, Electricity & Magnetism

Light Reflection & Refraction: Mirror formula $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$. Lens formula $\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$. Power of lens $P = \frac{1}{f\text{ (in meters)}}$.
Electricity: Ohm's Law $V = IR$. Series resistance $R_s = R_1 + R_2 + R_3$. Parallel resistance $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3}$. Joule's Law of Heating $H = I^2Rt$.
Magnetic Effects: Fleming's Left-Hand Rule determines force direction on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field.
